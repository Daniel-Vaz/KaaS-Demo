Exactly one worker holds the leader lease at a time (it runs the orphan-GC / metrics / health
sweeps); the others stand by and take over within seconds if it dies. To see who leads:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=worker | grep leader

so they are pinned to the node(s) matching kvm.local.nodeSelector, and the shell is capped at one
pod per such node. Use kvm.mode=remote for anything beyond a single-node/dev cluster.
